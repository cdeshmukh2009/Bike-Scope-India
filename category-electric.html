Electric Bikes

Smart urban mobility with low running cost and reduced emissions.

Who Should Buy

City riders with predictable daily distance and access to charging facilities.

Strengths

Instant torque, low maintenance requirements, and cleaner transportation.

Ultraviolette F77

The Ultraviolette F77 is a high-performance electric sports motorcycle developed by the Indian company Ultraviolette Automotive. It features an aggressive aerodynamic design and a powerful electric motor capable of delivering strong acceleration and sporty riding performance. The bike offers a claimed range of around 300 km depending on the variant and riding conditions. It also includes advanced technology such as ride modes, smartphone connectivity, traction control, and fast charging support, making it one of the most advanced electric motorcycles in India.

Revolt RV400

The Revolt RV400 is a popular electric commuter motorcycle produced by Revolt Motors. Designed mainly for daily city commuting, the RV400 offers a practical riding experience with a claimed range of about 150 km per charge. It comes with features like swappable battery technology, mobile app connectivity, multiple riding modes, and artificial exhaust sound options. With its affordable price and low running cost, the RV400 has become one of the most accessible electric motorcycles in India.

Ola Roadster

The Ola Roadster is a futuristic electric motorcycle developed by Ola Electric. It features a minimalist naked bike design combined with modern technology and performance-focused engineering. The Roadster lineup is expected to deliver strong acceleration, long riding range, and advanced smart features like a large touchscreen display, connected technology, and OTA software updates. With multiple variants planned, the Ola Roadster aims to make high-performance electric motorcycles more accessible for Indian riders.
